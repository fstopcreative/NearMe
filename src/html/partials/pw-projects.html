Which home improvements need a notice under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996

{{#if guideHref}} Party Wall guide {{/if}}

Rear extension

Needs notice if you build a new wall on or astride the boundary, or dig foundations within 3 m of a neighbour's building and deeper than theirs.

Sections 1 & 6Often

Side extension

Side returns and wrap-arounds usually sit on the boundary line and need new foundations right next to the neighbour's.

Sections 1 & 6Usually

Loft conversion

Cutting steel beams into the party wall, raising it or removing a shared chimney breast is work to a party wall.

Section 2Usually

Basement

Digging below your neighbour's foundations, and underpinning a shared wall, are both notifiable.

Sections 2 & 6Almost always

Excavation

Foundations, piling or deep drainage within 3 m of a neighbour's building, or within 6 m for deeper digs, can need notice.

Section 6Depends on depth

Structural alterations

Inserting beams into a shared wall, cutting in flashings or taking out a chimney breast on the party wall.

Section 2Usually
  • 1 month notice for new walls at the boundary and excavation
  • 2 months notice for work to an existing party wall
  • 14 days for the neighbour to reply
