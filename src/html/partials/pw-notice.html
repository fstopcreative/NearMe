Received a Party Wall notice?

Your neighbour in {{{areaHtml}}} is planning work

You have 14 days to reply. If you don't, a dispute is assumed and surveyors are appointed. Your three options:

  1. ConsentAgree to the work. A schedule of condition is still wise.
  2. Agreed surveyorDissent and share one impartial surveyor.
  3. Own surveyorDissent and appoint your own surveyor.

The building owner normally pays the surveyors' reasonable fees, and must make good any damage the works cause.
